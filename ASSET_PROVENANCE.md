The examples are explicitly labeled in `results.json` and `BENCHMARK.md`:

- Cases 01–02: VersionFixer artwork supplied for this project.
- Cases 03–04: newly AI-generated raster inputs, made with the built-in image-generation tool for this demonstration. They are not customer case studies, competitor outputs or artist testimonials.
- Cases 05–10: controlled geometric, typography, transparency, gradient, centerline and compression fixtures prepared for this test. They are not natural photographs or a representative evaluation dataset.
- Every “after” PNG is rendered from the corresponding actual engine-produced SVG. No retouching is used to improve the claimed output.
- Checkerboards in comparison cards are display aids for transparency; the raw input/output PNGs preserve their actual transparency.
- The overview and side-by-side JPEG cards contain labels and resized views. Use the separate original PNG and output SVG for close inspection.
